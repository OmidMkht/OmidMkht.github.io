I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Vermont, working with Mads R. Almassalkhi and Samuel Chevalier.

My research focuses on optimal network reduction for power systems, with applications to transmission and distribution networks.

## Research

My interests include power-system network reduction, optimization, sensor placement, and voltage estimation.

## Opti-KRON

[Opti-KRON](https://github.com/OmidMkht/Opti-KRON) uses optimization to reduce distribution-network models while controlling voltage error.
The framework includes methods for radial networks and unbalanced three-phase feeders.
